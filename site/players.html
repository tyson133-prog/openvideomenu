Players · Early access

Play menus from your own library.

ovmplayer apps play films and shows with Open Video Menu menus from Plex or Jellyfin on your TV. They're proof-of-concept players, built to show the format working today; the goal is for the apps you already use to support it natively.

TV app

ovmplayer for Google TV and Android TV

Browse your Plex or Jellyfin library, open titles straight into their menus, and play everything else as usual. Plays AC-3 and DTS soundtracks, and passes them through to your receiver where supported.

TV app

ovmplayer for Roku

The same menus on Roku TVs and players, with a native menu renderer. Roku plays one stream at a time, so a motion menu's music travels inside its video clip, and loops can pause very briefly at the loop point.

Your server

Share menus from Plex or Jellyfin

Players get menus from your media server. Nothing leaves your home network, and your media folders stay read-only.

Jellyfin plugin

Serves menus from your Jellyfin libraries directly, so there's nothing else to run. Apps find it automatically.

  1. Add the plugin repository in Dashboard → Plugins → Repositories: Repository URL
  2. Install Open Video Menu from the catalogue and restart Jellyfin.
  3. Put menus next to your films; the plugin finds them after each library scan.

Menu server for Plex

Plex doesn't take plugins, so a small read-only menu server runs beside it: Docker, a Linux service, or Node.js on Windows or macOS. It only answers people signed in to your Plex server, and apps find it automatically on your network.

# Docker, with your media mounted read-only
docker compose up -d
